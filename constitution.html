The Operational Framework of LEADPAC Foundation

THE CONSTITUTION OF LEADPAC FOUNDATION

15th December 2019 | 6.08am

Article 1.0: The Face of the Constitution

1.1 Preamble: The constitution of LEADPAC Foundation shall be known and called the Operational Constitution of LEADPAC Foundation.

The Constitution shall provide the Leadership, Organizational, Operational, Administrative, Managerial and Moral/Ethical framework upon which the organization is designed, led and administered daily in all geographical locations where LEADPAC Foundation is fully registered and operational.

The Constitution is designed flexibly to align itself with the rules and regulations governing Non-Governmental and Non-Profit Organization in any country the organization seeks registration and operation applying such guidelines within the laws of that country.

All formal requirements for the official registration, recognition and permits for free operations and public activities of the organization shall be met and signed to by the following leaders of LEADPAC Foundation viz: Board of Trustees Chairman, Board of Trustees Secretary, Country President, Country Secretary, the Executive Director, Africa, AYN Director of Operations, AYN Continental Admin Secretary, Country Assistant Executive Director and National Admin Secretary. These persons shall automatically become the Board of Trustees of LEADPAC Foundation in that country.

Ratification of major decisions bordering on the organization operations and activities in any country where LEADPAC Foundation is registered and operational shall only be confirmed and approved by the topmost leadership team of LEADPAC Foundation as stated in this constitution.

The Leadership, Managerial, Operational, Organizational and Administrative International Headquarters of the foundation shall be the geographical location where the idea first began – Calabar Municipality, Cross River State in Nigeria – West Africa.

All other African countries where the organization is fully registered and operational shall have their Country Offices and a Liaison Office at the Headquarters with a Country Representative as a Liaison Officer at the Organization Headquarters in Calabar Municipality, Cross River State in Nigeria, West Africa.

  • AGM: This is the annual assembly of all registered members, volunteers, partners and the Board of Directors in LEADPAC Foundation.

  • BOT: Board of Trustees drawn from within and outside LEADPAC
  • Good Governance: is the process and act of governing society and directing its government or state affairs to ensure good functioning of the political institutions and state organs to foster economic development, social cohesion, reduce corruption and guarantee full citizens participation, influence, oversight, responsive and responsible leadership for a harmonious societal development and transformation.

Article 3.0: The Idea

3.1 The Vision: To nurture and fulfil the dream of a transformed and prosperous nation

3.2 The Mission: To raise, grow, develop, teach, counsel, coach, train and mentor one million teenagers and youths in each state or province of a nation for the society and national leadership and entrepreneurial transformation.

3.3 The Goal: To raise generational CHANGE AGENTs across the nations of Africa with the New Leadership Idea of Vision – Purpose – Influence – Service - Sacrifice

3.4 The Slogan: We learn continuously and take small and practical steps towards true and effective leadership.

3.5 Our Core Belief System:

  • Growth Mindset: We believe in continuous learning and growth
  • Progress towards Perfection: We prioritize both progress and perfection in leadership, taking small steps progressively and consciously towards perfecting our quest for true and effective leadership.

3.6 The Overall Objectives:

  • To empower teenagers with positive thinking and self-awareness, self-discovery, personal identity and development.
  • To help them develop emotional intelligence and resilience
  • To promote healthy relationships with effective communication
  • To foster goal-setting, problem-solving and analytical skills
  • To build a team and network of young African leaders and entrepreneurs.
  • To encourage youth-led initiatives and community projects
  • To help young Africans develop practical innovative and creative skills in business development and planning, financial literacy, service delivery and marketing
  • To equip young Africans with leadership and management skills with entrepreneurial mindset
  • To encourage effective participation in socio-economic and political engagements in Africa and advocating for a corruption free society, true leadership and good governance.

3.7 Performance Indicators to evaluate success in LEADPAC Operations

  • Increased confidence and self-efficacy among members and participants in our projects and programs
  • Development of creative and innovative business ideas and start-up projects among young Africans
  • Improved leadership, entrepreneurial, managerial and problem-solving skills.
  • A growing network of young African leaders and entrepreneurs
  • Positive impacts on local communities through teens and youth-led initiatives
  • Increased employability and job-creation capacity among African youths in our organization and participants in our projects and programs.

3.8: Strategies and Plans to achieve LEADPAC Foundation Objectives:

Strategies:

  • Capacity Building: Provide training, mentorship and coaching to equip participants with true and effective leadership, management and entrepreneurial skills.
  • Networking and Partnerships: Collaborate with other relevant organizations, businesses and individuals to provide resources, funding and opportunities
  • Community Engagements: Engage with local communities to identify needs, promote the program and encourage participation.
  • Innovation and Incubation: Support the development of creative and innovative business ideas and provide incubation services

Plans:

  • Training Program: Develop a comprehensive training program covering leadership, entrepreneurship and business skills and mindset.
  • Mentorship Program: Match teens and young adults of African origin with experienced mentors and coaches for guidance and supports.
  • Business Plan Competition: Organize a competition to encourage participants to develop business plans and pitch ideas
  • Start-up Incubation: Provide resources and support for selected start-ups to develop and grow.

Projects:

  • Leadership Development Project: Organize workshops, training sessions, seminars, summit and conferences to develop true and effective leadership team with relevant developmental skills.
  • Entrepreneurship BootCamp: Host a bootcamp to equip participants with practical entrepreneurial growth skills.
  • Innovation Challenge: Launch a challenge to encourage young Africans to develop innovative sustainable solutions for real-African problems.
  • Community Outreach Project: Engage with local communities and Community Based Organization (CBO) to promote our programs and support community development especially in education and healthcare among others.

Programs:

  • Youth Leadership Training Program: A comprehensive program to develop leadership skills among young people in Africa.
  • Entrepreneurship Training Program: A program to equip young Africans with entrepreneurial skills and support startup growth
  • Mentorship Program: A program to match teenagers and young adults with experienced mentors and coaches.
  • Alumni Network: A program to connect program graduates and provide ongoing support and networking opportunities.

Article 4.0 LEADPAC Membership

4.1 Membership: The organization is a teen and youth-based network for Africans only. Membership shall be Africans between the ages of 10 to 30 years within our specified leadership growth range and demographics.

All interested member at all levels of the organization shall be required to apply for registration to the local branch of LEADPAC foundation in their respective countries. Only registered and fully trained individual across the growth ranks shall hold an Executive office and/or function at the top leadership level of the Organization both at the Local, State, National and the Continental organs of LEADPAC Foundation.

The registration fee shall be determined by the individual country leadership and shall not be less than one US Dollar equivalent in local currency.

4.2: Forfeiture of Membership: An individual shall cease to be a member of LEADPAC Foundation in any country where the organization is fully registered and operational if his/her conduct, attitude relationship, associations, business transaction and public service engagements are found to be inimical and injurious to the goal and objectives of LEADPAC Foundation referred to in Article 4.0 (including 4.1 to 4.6). Evidence of involvement in cultism, confraternities, corruption practices, moral bankruptcy, violence of any kind, sponsorship of terrorism, encouragement and/or practice of any crime recognized in the country where LEADPAC Foundation is registered and operational shall render your membership automatically invalid and null and void. Such individual or group of persons shall at that instant lose all rights and privileges enjoyed by members of the organization

Article 5.0: Composition of Board of Trustees

5.1: First Board of the Organization: The Board of Trustees shall be made up of 9 members who shall be drawn from within and outside LEADPAC Foundation. This group shall be responsible for the registration of LEADPAC Foundation across the 55 African Countries. They shall be individual who have shown track records in true and effective leadership, known for their honesty and integrity in public affairs and have maximum interest in; and supporting teenagers and young adults’ overall growth and development in leadership and entrepreneurship skills, promoting and advocating good governance, gender equality, human rights advocacy and practice, family growth, peace and stability and the obedient to the rule of law in the society.

5.2: Second Board of the Organization: This group shall be known and called the Junior Board of Trustees of LEADPAC Foundation. Members of this Board shall be 10 leaders within the LEADPAC Family particularly AYN Top leaders selected from different African countries and drawn strictly from within the leadership structure of the organization. Their responsibility primarily shall be engagements with donor agencies and partners and the administrative advice on the organization funds especially from donor agencies and supports from partners.

5.3: Tenure of Office: The Board Chairman, Deputy Chairman and Secretary shall be elected from among members at its inaugural session by simple majority for a period not exceeding 5 years. The Board Chairman and other officials may be eligible for re-election for a second term of 5 years.

5.4: Quorum of the Board: Two third of 9 persons, (that’s 6persons) shall be allow to hold a meeting and all resolutions and decisions taken shall be binding on all members of the Board. At the Junior Board, two third of 10, (that’s 7 persons) shall be considered to have form a quorum in any meeting; and decisions and resolutions shall be valid and binding of all the members.

5.5: Board Decisions Making: The two Boards shall reach all decisions and resolutions by consensus, but if and when necessary, a vote shall be taken on any important issue(s) which vote will be carried by a simple majority. The Board Chairman shall have a casting vote. The decision for the regulation of the manner in which the meetings of the Board shall be conducted shall rest on the Board.

5.6: Board Meetings: The first Board of Trustees shall meet twice in a year excluding emergency meeting where necessary. The Junior Board shall meet 4 times in a year – that’s once in every quarter. The last meeting shall hold at least not later than 30days before the organization AGM.

5.7: Responsibilities and Functions of the Board of Trustees: The Board of Trustees shall be responsible for:-

  • The leadership training of the topmost leadership, administrative and management team of the LEADPAC Foundation
  • The raising of funds for LEADPAC Foundation
  • Holding all the properties and resources of LEADPAC Foundation in trust
  • The Chairman through the Board Secretary shall cause a meeting of the Board to be convened with at least a 7day notice to the meeting.
  • In a situation or circumstance where the Chairman fails or is unable to convene a meeting when due or necessary, 2/3 majority of the Board of Trustees shall cause a meeting of the BOT to be convened through the Board Secretary and all decisions and resolutions reached in such a meeting shall be valid and binding on all the members.
  • Notice of an ordinary or scheduled meeting including emergency of the Board shall be in writing transmitted either electronically and/or manually; specifying the time, place and agenda to be used at the meeting not less than 7 days before the meeting, except for emergencies and special meetings.
  • The Board Chairman shall preside over all the meetings and in his/her absence, the Deputy Chairman of the Board shall preside.
  • Subject to the approval of the Board, the Chairman through the Secretary may invite any person providing such invitation is not inimical to the objectives of the organization to attend a meeting of the Board and take part in its deliberations, but such a person shall not be entitled to the right of voting and decision-making process of the Board.

Article 6.0: LEADPAC Foundation Meetings

6.1: The General Assembly: This is the gathering of all registered members of the organization, volunteers, partners and members of the Board of Trustees. The meeting shall be held once in a year. This gathering is otherwise refers to as LEADPAC Family Square. The Country President assisted by the Executive Director and the Country Secretary shall jointly host the Family Square. During the LEADPAC Family Square, the department of I Love Africa shall be operational with all the culinary activities bordering on mostly native African cuisines. The Family Square shall be in the form of a picnic, an excursion, a sit-out and/or a banquet. With the approval of the Country President, other non-members of the organization may be invited to the event.

6.2: Annual General Meeting – AGM: The LEADPAC Foundation Annual General Meeting shall be the highest business and operational session of the organization. It shall be a delegate meeting from all the countries where LEADPAC Foundation is fully registered and operational.

  • The AGM of LEADPAC Foundation shall be held on the Founder’s Day of the organization for Performance Evaluation of the calendar year’s projects and programs and review of projected projects and programs in the new calendar year.
  • The AGM shall review all existing appointments and partnerships with the view to strengthening the bond, replace non-functional leaders, review responsibilities of individuals and teams and assign special tasks to both individuals and teams.
  • The AGM shall ratify all submitted budgets and harmonize all financial projections to provide for a single financial estimate in line with all the projects, programs and other projected expenditures to have LEADPAC Budget for the year.
  • Notice of the meeting in form of a written letter shall be sent to the appointed delegates as specify in this constitution 6 months – 180 days prior to the Annual General Meeting.
  • The Annual General Meeting – AGM shall be presided by the Country President of LEADPAC Foundation. In his absence, the Executive Director – Africa shall preside over the meeting.

6.3: AGM Delegates: The delegates shall be the topmost leaders of the LEADPAC Foundation including, but not limited to the Country President, Executive Director-Africa, Country Secretary, Assistant Executive Directors, National Admin Secretaries, AYN Director of Operations, AYN Continental Admin Secretary, staff in the Executive Director, Africa, Country Secretary and AYN Operations’ offices, LEADPAC Communities Leaders and their Secretaries in both Ignite, Teens’ Lead Africa and African Youths Network. There shall be LEADPAC Foundation in all the 55+ countries within the African Continent. This is the Pan-African Initiative to drive true and effective leadership development dream among teenagers and young African adults towards a better Africa and changing the Africa unpleasant narratives.

  • Communication and Liaison: Serve as a liaison between the Executive Director, Board, and staff, and represent the organization externally.
  • Compliance and Risk Management: Ensure compliance with regulations, laws, and organizational policies.

The role requires strong leadership, communication, and problem-solving skills, as well as experience in nonprofit management and fundraising.

Other responsibilities shall include: -

  • Ensuring compliance with local laws and regulations
  • Driving fundraising efforts in respective countries
  • Reporting progress and updates to the Executive Director, Africa at the Headquarters of the organization and stakeholders.

  • Assisting with logistics for programs and activities
  • Keeping records and doing data entry

12.9 National Coordinators: Within the Country, there shall be the National Coordinators for the three LEADPAC Foundation Communities viz: Ignite, Teens Lead Africa and African Youths Network.

As a National Coordinator in a teens' and young adults' NGO – LEADPAC Foundation in a country like Nigeria, responsibilities might include:

  • Overseeing national programs and activities for teens and youths
  • Coordinating with local teams, partners, and stakeholders
  • Ensuring programs align with organization’s goals and meet teens and youth’s needs
  • Managing resources, budgets, and reporting for national operations
  • Representing the organization at national events or with partners.

12.10 LEADPAC Legal Adviser: LEADPAC Foundation shall have a Legal Adviser.

12.12 Legal Status of LEADPAC: The Organization shall be a distinct legal entity with the power to acquire, hold and alienate property of any kind and with the power to acquire and incur obligations and have perpetual succession.

12.13 LEADPAC Partners, Donors & Sponsors: The Organization shall partner with other relevant agencies and bodies for the execution of her projects and programs and shall seek supports and collaborations from these institutions and establishments where necessary.

13.0 Non-Profit-Making Status

13.1 LEADPAC Foundation is not formed and does not exist for the purpose of carrying on any business that has as its object the making of gain or profit.

13.2 The income and assets of LEADPAC Foundation shall be applied solely for the promotion of the objects for which it is established.

13.3 No part of the income or assets of LEADPAC Foundation shall be paid directly or indirectly by way of dividend to any person or organization.

14.0 LEADPAC Finance

14.1 The income of the organization shall be derived from sponsorships, registration, dues, aids, donations, interest on investments and savings.

14.2 The financial year of LEADPAC Foundation shall be from 1st of January to 31st of December,2026.

14.3 All monies received by or on behalf of LEADPAC Foundation shall be paid into the approved LEADPAC Foundation accounts within 48 hours to 72 hours where there are intervening public holidays or weekends.

14.4 Full and accurate books and records shall be kept by management for all financial transactions of the communities.

14.5 There shall be a (5) five-member finance team comprising of an accountant, the Executive Director, Africa, the BOT Secretary, Junior Board Chairman and one member of the organization.

14.6 There shall be three (3) signatories to all LEADPAC Foundation accounts; and the Country President in category A must sign with any of category B or C.

Resignation from LEADPAC Foundation:

14.7 All resignation from the Board of Trustees shall be signed and addressed to the Board Chairman of the BOT through the Board Secretary.

14.8 In the case of the resignation of the Board Chairman, he/she shall address the letter to the Deputy Board Chairman through the Board Secretary.

14.9 In the case of the Board Secretary, he/she shall write to the BOT Chairman after which and emergency BOT and LEADPAC Foundation Leadership and Management Team shall convened a meeting.

14.10 In the case of the resignation of any other officer(s) across all the teams and units in LEADPAC Foundation in all the countries where the organization is fully registered and operational, all resignations shall be addressed to the Country Secretary and a copy sent to the relevant team heads.

15.0 Amendments to the Constitution

LEADPAC Foundation may decide by a resolution of two third of the Annual General Assembly – AGM vote in favour or against the amendment of any provision(s) of this Constitution.

16.0 Dissolution of LEADPAC Foundation

16.1 LEADPAC Foundation shall cease to operate if at least two third of her members vote to dissolve because of activities and/or actions that is inimical to the fundamental goal and objectives of the organization.

16.2 The Board of Trustees shall convene an emergency meeting to evaluate the vote for or against the dissolution and shall confirm and approved or disapproved the action.

16.3 In the event of a dissolution, all the assets of LEADPAC Foundation remaining after the discharge of liabilities shall be disposed of by the Board of Trustees in such a manner as the Board may by unanimous resolution determine, provided that no property whatsoever shall be given to any individuals, but it shall be given or transferred to some other organizations involved in teenagers and young adults leadership and entrepreneurship development and soft-skills training and which similarly prohibits the distribution of their assets amongt her members or trustees or outside whatsoever the case may be.
