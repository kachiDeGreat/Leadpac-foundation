About

Teens' Lead Africa Team focused on Discovery of Purpose and training in Business Development and Entrepreneurship Skills. The Team is all about empowering young people to discover their purpose and equipping them with the skills to turn their ideas into successful businesses.

It is nurturing the next generation of entrepreneurs and leaders in Africa. They're like a launchpad for young talent, helping them develop the skills and mindset needed to thrive in business and make a positive impact.

To achieve their goals, they will be involved in various projects, programs, and activities, including:

  • Entrepreneurship Training: Providing business management skills, mentorship, and hands-on experience in market research, financial management, and leadership.
  • STEM and Entrepreneurship Programs: Offering immersive innovation camps, seed funding, and mentorship to young entrepreneurs, especially girls, in science, technology, engineering, and math (STEM) fields.
  • Leadership Development: Organizing workshops, conferences, and summits to develop leadership skills, promote self-discovery, and foster entrepreneurship among youth.
  • Youth Entrepreneurship and Innovation Multi-Donor Trust Fund: Supporting youth-led start-ups and entrepreneurship ecosystem growth in Africa, with a focus on women entrepreneurs.
  • Business Management Training: Providing comprehensive training on financial management, marketing, sales, operations, and leadership to equip young entrepreneurs with essential skills.
  • Innovation Challenges and Competitions: Hosting competitions and hackathons to encourage innovation, teamwork, and problem-solving among young entrepreneurs.
  • Mentorship and Networking: Offering mentorship, networking opportunities, and access to funding, investors, and industry experts to support young entrepreneurs.

The above listed projects, programs, and activities aim to empower young Africans to become successful entrepreneurs, leaders, and change-makers in their communities.

There are specific skills that are essential for young entrepreneurs to succeed in Africa?

  • Financial literacy and management
  • Digital skills (e.g., e-commerce, social media marketing)
  • Problem-solving Skills and Adaptability
  • Communication and Networking Skills
  • Leadership and Team Management Skills
  • Innovation and Creativity Skills
  • Resilience and Risk-taking Skills

These skills we believe will help young African entrepreneurs navigate the challenges of doing business in Africa and capitalize on opportunities in the continent's growing markets.

Some promising sectors available for young entrepreneurs in Africa include:

  • Agriculture and agribusiness
  • Fintech and financial services
  • Healthcare and wellness
  • Renewable energy and sustainability
  • E-commerce and digital services
  • Education and skills development

These sectors offer opportunities for innovation, job creation, and impact, and align with Africa's development priorities.

We create an environment for extra-curricular activities providing a great way for Teens' to have fun, learn new skills, and build relationships. Some of the activities are:-

  • Sports and Fitness: soccer, basketball, volleyball, swimming, dance, martial arts.
  • Creative Expression: music, art, drama, writing, photography, videography.
  • Clubs and Groups: debate team, public speaking, chess, robotics, coding, entrepreneurship.
  • Community Service: volunteering, community outreach, environmental projects.
  • Cultural Events: cultural festivals, talent shows, fashion shows, food festivals
  • Business Plan Competitions: develop and pitch business ideas
  • Shark Tank-style Pitching: pitch ideas to investors.
  • Startup Bootcamp: develop business ideas.
  • Leadership Retreats: leadership development and team-building.
  • Industry Visits: tours and insights from industry experts.
  • Guest Speaker Series: inspiring entrepreneurs and industry leaders
  • Business-themed games: Monopoly, The Sims, Stock Market Challenge
  • Strategy games: Chess, Scrabble, Risk.
  • Team-building games: Escape Room, Scavenger Hunt, Human Knot.
  • Problem-solving games: Puzzles, Brain Teasers, Logic Games.
  • Entrepreneurship challenges: Pitch Day, Business Plan Competition, Shark Tank simulation

Specific Games to spice the training:

  • "The Business Game": simulate running a business.
  • "Marketing Mashup": create marketing campaigns.
  • "Financial Literacy Challenge": manage finances.
  • "Leadership Showdown": leadership and teamwork challenges.
  • "Innovation Quest": develop innovative solutions

These games has help our teenagers develop skills like strategic thinking, problem-solving, teamwork, and communication while having fun!

We also believe fitness activities are a great way to boost energy and focus, so we create some fitness channels for our Teens'.

  • Fitness classes: yoga, Zumba, aerobics, strength training.
  • Outdoor activities: hiking, camping, outdoor games, nature walks.
  • Fun challenges: fitness competitions, obstacle courses, dance-offs
  • "Fit Frenzy": morning fitness sessions.
  • "Wellness Wednesdays": wellness workshops.
  • "Sports Day": inter-school sports tournament.
  • "Fitness Scavenger Hunt": fitness challenges.
  • "Mindful Moments": meditation and mindfulness sessions.
