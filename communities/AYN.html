About

African Youths Network Team focuses on Functional/Historical African Leadership, Socio-Political Engagement and Economic Growth and Development. The AYN Team is a dynamic group aiming at empowering young Africans to become Change Agents on the Continental Stage.

Members are open to all young Africans from the ages of 19 to 30 including graduands from LEADPAC 🤝 Teens' Business and Management School, Undergraduates, College Students and young professionals (adults) who are interested in changing the Africa narratives.

AYN is all about:

  • Functional/Historical African Leadership: exploring Africa's rich history and leadership traditions
  • Socio-Political Engagement: encouraging active citizenship and participation in shaping Africa's future through effective leadership ideas and good governance.
  • Economic Growth and Development: promoting entrepreneurship, innovation, and economic empowerment

AYN team is often liken to a catalyst designed for positive change; equipping young Africans with the knowledge, skills, and networks to drive Africa's growth and development.

Engagement are on:-

  • Leadership development
  • Civic engagement
  • Economic empowerment
  • Pan-Africanism
  • Youth-led initiatives
  • Climate Change Work
  • Healthcare Functional Education
  • Creativity and Innovation to create opportunities

AYN activities involves:

  • Leadership training and mentorship
  • Community outreach and engagement
  • Entrepreneurship support and innovation
  • Policy advocacy and research
  • Networking and partnerships

AYN team is on a mission to unlock Africa's potential and create a brighter future for all!

The Mindset Shift Project is Africa developmental game-changer. Our choices Projects, Programs, and activities are:-

Projects:

  • Leadership Bootcamps: intensive training on leadership, entrepreneurship, and mindset shift
  • Youth Empowerment Forums: platforms for young Africans to share experiences and ideas
  • Community Development Initiatives: projects addressing specific community needs (e.g., education, healthcare)
  • Mentorship Programs: pairing young Africans with experienced leaders and entrepreneurs
  • Innovation Challenges: competitions encouraging innovative solutions to African challenges

Programs:

  • Mindset Shift Curriculum: developing and implementing a curriculum for schools and youth organizations.
  • Youth Leadership Development Program: comprehensive program for young leaders
  • Entrepreneurship Training: equipping young Africans with entrepreneurship skills.
  • Civic Engagement Program: encouraging young Africans to participate in socio-political processes.
  • Pan-African Exchange Program: exchange programs, study tours, and cultural immersion

Activities:

  • Workshops and Seminars: mindset shift, leadership, entrepreneurship, and personal development.
  • Conferences and Summits: platforms for knowledge sharing and networking.
  • Hackathons and Ideathons: innovative solution development.
  • Volunteer Opportunities: community service and social impact projects.
  • Online Campaigns: online awareness and engagement campaigns on mindset shift and youth empowerment.

AYN goal is to inspire, empower, and equip young Africans with the mindset and skills to drive positive change in the continent.

The Mindset Shift Curriculum is a transformative program designed to empower young Africans with a positive, growth-oriented mindset. It's a comprehensive framework that helps youth overcome limiting beliefs, develop self-awareness, and unlock their potential.

Key Components:

  • Self-Discovery: understanding values, strengths, and passions
  • Mindset Reboot: shifting from fixed to growth mindset.
  • Goal Setting: setting SMART goals and creating action plans.
  • Resilience Building: developing coping strategies and perseverance.
  • Leadership Development: building leadership skills and confidence.
  • Entrepreneurial Mindset: fostering innovation and creativity

AYN Anticipated Learning Outcomes:

  • Develop a positive self-image and confidence
  • Cultivation of Growth Mindsets and Resilience
  • Ability to det and achieve personal and professional goals
  • Enabling critical thinking and problems solving creatively
  • Ability to lead and inspire others with purpose and passion

AYN Deliverables:

  • Interactive workshops and seminars
  • Online modules and resources
  • Mentorship and coaching
  • Peer learning and group discussions
  • Real-world projects and challenges

Anticipated Impact:

  • Empowers young Africans to take ownership of their lives
  • Equips them with skills and mindset for success
  • Fosters a community of positive change-makers
  • Contributes to Africa's socio-economic development

The Mindset Shift Curriculum is a powerful tool for transforming the lives of young Africans and shaping the continent's future!
