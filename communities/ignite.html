About

Our Ignite Team is a dynamic and interactive program designed specifically for teenagers between the ages of 13 to 18; aiming to spark a passion for personal leadership development and academic excellence!

The program focuses on empowering teens with essential life skills, confidence, and mindset shifts to excel in academics and beyond. Through engaging workshops, mentorship, and hands-on activities, participants will:

  • Discover their strengths and passions
  • Develop a growth mindset and resilience
  • Set and achieve academic and personal goals
  • Build leadership skills and confidence
  • Cultivate a supportive community of like-minded peers

Our Teens' Training Session make use of the dominance thoughts and life challenges of teenagers to help them overcome these obstacles.

  • Self-discovery and identity: Self-discovery and identity: Figuring out who they are, what they stand for, and where they fit in.
  • Peer pressure and social media influence: Navigating social relationships, bullying, and online presence.
  • Academic stress and pressure: Managing workload, grades, and future plans.
  • Body changes and self-image: - Coping with physical changes, self-acceptance, and self-esteem.
  • Independence vs. parental expectations: Balancing freedom, rules, and parental guidance.
  • Future uncertainty: Exploring career options, college, and life after high school.

Some common thoughts we have discovered that dominate teenagers' minds are: What if I fail?, What do others think of me?, Am I good enough?, I don't fit in, I can't do this.

These thoughts and challenges impact their mental health, relationships, and overall well-being

Our strategies includes:-

  1. Active listening: Hear them out, and validate their emotions
  2. Guided self-reflection: Help them identify their strengths, weaknesses, and interests
  3. Positive affirmations: Encourage self-compassion and challenge negative self-talk
  4. Goal-setting: Support them in setting achievable goals and celebrating progress
  5. Role-playing: Practice scenarios to build confidence and skills
  6. Mentorship: Pair them with positive role models or mentors
  7. Mindfulness and self-care: Teach techniques to manage stress and prioritize well-being

We also provide resources and workshops on:

  • Self-awareness and self-acceptance
  • Building resilience and coping skills
  • Effective communication and relationships
  • Time management and organization
  • Goal-setting and future planning

Self-discovery and Self Leadership Session takes into the training session the following:

Self-Discovery:

  1. Goal-setting: Set SMART goals and create action plans
  2. Self-awareness: Recognize emotions, thoughts, and behaviors
  3. Self-regulation: Develop strategies to manage emotions and behaviors
  4. Self-motivation: Identify intrinsic motivators and celebrate progress
  5. Accountability: Establish accountability partnerships or mentors

Some guiding questions:

Other questions the answer are:-
What are my strengths and passions?

  • What are your strengths and passions?
  • What are your values and priorities?
  • What kind of leader do we want to raise in them?
  • How can you take ownership of your life and choices?

Other possible activities are:- Strength:

  • Strengths assessment (e.g., CliftonStrengths)
  • Passion project brainstorming
  • Skills inventory (e.g., writing, public speaking, problem-solving)

What are my values and priorities?

  • What matters most to me in life?
  • What do I stand for?
  • What kind of relationships do I want to build?
  • Values card sort
  • Writing a personal mission statement
  • Reflecting on role models or heroes

What kind of leader do I want to be?

  • What leadership qualities do I admire?
  • How do I want to impact others?
  • What kind of leader do I need to be to achieve my goals?

Leadership Development:

  • Leadership style self-assessment
  • Role-playing different leadership scenarios
  • Creating a leadership vision board

How can I take ownership of my life and choices?

  • What are my goals and priorities?
  • What steps can I take to achieve them?
  • How will I hold myself accountable?
