About Us

LEADPAC 🤝 Foundation is Pan-African Leadership and Entrepreneurship Development and Training initiative aimed at shifting the mindset of young African Youths and Teenagers from the prevalent consumption mentality to productivity and deployment of their potentials, gifts and acquired skills for society transformation.

What We Do

We practically and strategically move young minds - African Teens' and Youths (13-30)from the place of comfort and consumption mentality to the place of Productivity and a Shift in Mindset with the aim of helping them rediscover purpose, deploying hidden potentials and applying acquired skills to solving simple, diverse, complex and challenging leadership and Entrepreneurship problems in Africa for Africans.

Vision

To nurture and fulfill the dream of a transformed and prosperous nation using the new effective leadership idea to raise Change Agents

Mission Statement

To grow, raise, develop, coach, train, counsel and mentor on million teenagers and young adults in each state of the federation for society and national transformation

"...we are taking small steps to grow, cultivate, practice and mature into true and effective leadership"


LEADPAC 🤝 Core Belief System

Growth Mindset: We believe in continuous learning and growth.

Progress towards Perfection: We prioritize both progress and perfection in leadership, taking small steps progressively and consciously towards perfecting our quest for true and effective leadership.
